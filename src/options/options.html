Story Studio Audio Generator — Settings

Appearance
Story Studio connection

Use localhost when Story Studio runs on this computer. For a remote or Tailscale server, grant site access, then sign in.

Sign in is only needed when your remote Story Studio server requires credentials.

Finished audio is saved back to the connected Story Studio project.

Fleet Hub

Reports this extension’s local queue health to your Fleet Hub. It never gives the Hub direct provider access.

The pairing secret stays in this browser profile and signs each Hub request and response.

Hub controls are limited to pause-after-current, resume of a Hub pause, clear pending, and selected normal-failure retries. Provider, voice, pacing, cooldown, credentials, and site permissions remain local.
